## Source behavior preserved within the bounded M05 fixture

The original Return is a **new linked record**, not a modification to a purchase. It consumes only **the original purchase's remaining stock lots**, then reduces shop stock, records a source-linked **outgoing** Stock Movement, and records a Cash Book **Credit** (supplier hands money back to the shop). It does **not** change the Supplier's Current Due or its supplier ledger for a Cash refund. A return with method `Supplier Credit` is a different future slice.

The original `supplierPurchaseCashPaid_` reconstructs verifiable paid amount from initial purchase and later source-linked supplier settlements. Its cash-backed method set includes **Cash, Bank, Mobile Banking and historical blank cash**; the Cash-refund ceiling is `cash-backed verified paid - earlier Cash Purchase Returns`. The synthetic M05 purchase fixture has only immutable original `paid_paisa` and Cash/Bank initial tender; **no later settlement provenance**. This proof accepts original Cash/Bank paid amounts as verifiable but **does not infer later settlements**. An unpaid original purchase cannot receive a Cash refund even if Supplier Due is positive.

Original `m05_lots.qty` is the historical **purchased quantity** and schema restricts it to >0. Therefore the new proof preserves the original M05 lot and purchase receipts byte-for-byte, and uses **disposable** `m07_purchase_lot_balances.remaining` as the source-lot remaining quantity (the production M05/M07 integration owner must define its one authoritative remaining-stock source). The original purchase/source lot/line, movement and Supplier ID must match, along with reviewed Supplier→Party source link. No borrowing from another purchase/supplier's lot even when overall shop stock is sufficient. The economic per-unit refund is based on original `grand_paisa / qty` (after invoice discount); amounts not **exactly divisible into paisa** for this whole-unit subset are **DECLINED**, not guessed or rounded. This leaves source's broader pricing/rounding parity unresolved.
