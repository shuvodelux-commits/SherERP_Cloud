## 12-sentence overview

M08 already has source-verified read projections for Owner, Lender, Borrower, and raw Customer/Supplier ledgers. These projections lack a combined tenant-scoped real local D1 proof that keeps original source identities separate. This slice adds isolated STRICT test tables with `m08_role_*` prefixes rather than changing production schema. Read-only parameterized local D1 snapshots will project Finance Person masters, finance transactions, and raw Customer/Supplier ledger rows. Original Finance Person IDs are authoritative; matching name or phone never merges two persons. A single person can hold Owner, Lender, and Borrower balances simultaneously without automatic cross-role offsets. Opening Owner Capital, Opening Loan, and Opening Borrower Loan change historical account positions without moving Cash Book or Bank Book. Negative Owner advances, Lender credits, and Borrower credits stay separate as six gross summary buckets. Deleted finance transactions and deleted Party ledger rows must not contribute to current balances. Customer and Supplier ledgers remain distinct even if their legacy IDs match, with source business-date and physical-row order preserved. Duplicate, blank, unresolved or noncanonical original identities fail closed rather than being guessed or silently ignored. Tests compare results with the pinned GAS oracle and unchanged D1 snapshots; M03 remains sole owner of live posting, SQL-atomic auth, migration, and any eventual settlement.

## Design and safety

- Use exact per-tenant `legacy_row` order for Finance Person/transaction records and Party Ledger rows; imported cents are safe integers, converted to source amounts for the existing pure M08 calculators only. SQLite queries bind tenant, and do not use `user` or global data as a fallback.
- `previewD1RoleAccountIsolation(DB,{tenantId,customerId,supplierId})` accepts strict plain object with nonblank exact identifiers; both Party roles may share one literal ID but their balances cannot offset. Return `{finance,customer,supplier}` from existing `reconcileFinancePersonBalances` and `projectRawLegacyPartyLedger`.
- For the Finance Person source, source-shaped fields include `Person ID`, `Person Name`, `Person Type`, `Roles`, `Status`, `Phone`, `Description`; for finance, `Finance ID`, `Person ID`, `Person`, `Transaction Type`, `Amount`, `Is Deleted`. The pure reconciliation rejects orphan IDs and unknown types. Hard-fail duplicate source `Finance ID`, even if an imported row is deleted; avoid audit ambiguity.
- Raw Party row identity is `Ledger ID` within its role; preserve `_rowIndex`, `Customer ID` versus `Supplier ID`, debit/credit sign and source deletion flag. Reject unknown role/deletion-state conversions before projection. Opening balances are record types, not instructions to touch the Cash Session.
- No name/phone alias, cross-role settlement, public finance API, real D1 migration, remote data access, writable endpoint or actual financial side effect. M02-P legacy identity and gross mapping, M03 posting, M04 authorization, and M06 payment integration remain release blockers. This is one M08 owner-scoped read-only proof, not a release approval.
