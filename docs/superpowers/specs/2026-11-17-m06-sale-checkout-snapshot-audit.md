## Proposed isolated contract
New pure internal `auditMixedUnitSaleCheckoutSnapshot(preview)` consumes **only** the already-created checkout preview `{lines,subtotal,discount,grandTotal,allocation}` and returns `{ready,issues,summary}`. It does not fetch today’s catalog or recompute FIFO/planned sales. It reports deterministic issue codes and line indexes, without inserting/updating rows, changing the input, or suggesting data repair. Plain, accessor-free source snapshots are required.

For each unique source Product ID, validate positive base quantity and exact-paisa nonnegative line amount, valid `Own|Negative|Vendor` shortage mode, unique positive per-line FIFO Lot ID allocations, and `sum(FIFO draws)+shortfall=baseQty` (within 1e-7 base-unit precision). `Own` must have zero shortfall. For mixed-unit lines, require aligned, immutable historical `unitBreakdownSnapshot` and `unitPricingSnapshot` for every distinct conversion ID; match conversionId/unitName/factor/qty, positive factor and qty, valid nonnegative price, and `sum(factor*qty)=baseQty`. A legacy single-unit line must have **both** unit arrays empty, not a half-present invented breakdown.

**Exact money rules:** Sum the authoritative saved lineTotal in integer paisa; require subtotal equals it, and `grandTotal=max(0,subtotal-discount)` in integer paisa. Verify original checkout `paidAmount+dueAmount=grandTotal`, sum of applied child tenders equals paidAmount, per-row tender/apply/change rules, unique payment-row IDs, and Cash change equals recorded changeAmount. Do not infer Customer/Party identity from the invoice. **Never assert `baseQty*unitPrice=lineTotal`**: effective base prices are rounded for display and may differ from exact component totals (e.g. 145 Pcs at displayed ৳9.24, actual line total ৳1,340). For component pricing, compare the sum `qty*unitPrice` to authoritative lineTotal **only within a bound of 0.005 per component unit quantity plus 0.01 per line**, accounting for original component-rate rounding; this is a consistency review, not authoritative money proof.

**Output:** `issues` array of `{code,line}` with no raw balances/IDs. Summary contains counts and conservative numeric original totals only. Malformed plain-object/array/accessor shapes throw a TypeError. Semantic corruption yields `ready:false`; never auto-fix. This is an audit of an internal preview candidate, **not** M07 reversal authority or D1 import/posting acceptance.
