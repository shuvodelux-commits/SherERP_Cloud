**Status:** owner-approved M07 continuation only as test-only evidence. Original source `Mohim_ERP@29aa4a5c7a7062a49e6bf4758beda3a8d62d9ff2` remains unchanged.

Source `SalesReturn.gs` commits Store Credit by subtracting exact return amount from selected Customer `Current Due`, allowing a **negative signed balance** and writing a source-linked `CUSTOMER_LEDGER` Credit with that `Balance`. It posts **no Cash Book or Bank Book** event. Named walk-in Store Credit can resolve/create a Customer in old GAS; Cloudflare migration must not invent a Party without M02-P review.

**Design:** Extend the already disposable local M07/M06 D1 proof tables with one synthetic signed Customer subaccount/credit ledger and new Store Credit receipt type, requiring exact original `m06_invoices.customer_id`, reviewed `m06_party_links`, matching signed account Party, original invoice hash/stock lot, in-transaction session+quantity+price guards. The signed account begins at the original verified M06 due in this isolated test only. After each Store Credit it is **the test's signed truth**; M06's nonnegative due schema is left unchanged to demonstrate why production M08 owns the reconciled account transition. Single SQLite insertion/trigger reserves returned lot quantity, restores shop/lot, inserts movement, subtracts signed Customer balance and records source-linked ledger row. Unique tenant+operation and stable M03 intent hash guard replay; cross-method Cash/Store Credit reuse is rejected even when there is physical stock left. No auto settlement to supplier/vendor/loan/equity.

**Accept:** real local Workerd/D1 covers positive due decrement, negative advance on paid sale, correct Customer vs other identities and Supplier AP, repeat/changed intent, concurrent last returned unit, cross-method IDs, closed Cash Session, forged source lot, stale customer link, injected ledger/stock failure rollback. Original Cash method stays unchanged. Test Worker has no POST route and no production schema/Worker/RPC change.

**Non-goals:** M04 authenticated actor, multi-invoice batch, legacy walk-in customer creation, actual M05 lot provenance, source-grade FIFO/vendor/OVERSOLD/bundles, cash-session policy beyond simple fixture, true M08 Party account schema, later Customer settlement/reversal, original browser or backdate rules. These remain blocked under `PARALLEL-MODULE-READY=false`. This is neither a second production accounting engine nor a readiness claim.
