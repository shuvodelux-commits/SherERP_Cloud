## Intent and design
The saved original `allocation` header must be independently consistent with the saved child rows, without accessing current payment accounts or rewriting historical totals. The exact native helper returns `paymentType = Cash | Partial | Due`, `paymentStatus = Paid | Partial | Due`, a one-positive-row `paymentMethod` and corresponding `bankAccountId/bankAccountName`, or `Multiple` and blank account fields when multiple positive rows exist. Audit must reject forged paymentType, method, account attribution, duplicate/missing/out-of-range `rowOrder`, unsupported zero/tender rows, and a nonempty Bank ID on Cash. Preserve a legal 0-applied Cash row only when the Cash tender is returned entirely as change and Bank/Mobile already covers the invoice. Multiple payments may have nonconsecutive rowOrder when a zero-amount input was filtered out. Reject empty `stockUnit` on original Sale Item as independent historical evidence corruption. Do not require the rounded effective base `unitPrice` times baseQty to equal lineTotal.

The implementation extends **only** M06-owned `src/modules/m06-sale-checkout-snapshot-audit.js`, adds `tests/m06-checkout-payment-envelope.test.mjs` and this plan, no new storage schema, no existing M05/M03 code, no public endpoint, no Cash Session, no authorizing a write. The audit remains a diagnostic of a trusted, server-supplied internal preview candidate. It returns deterministic `{ready,issues,summary}` without exposing new raw IDs, private costs, actor/session data or repair instructions. A future M03 kernel must recompute stock, split tender, current authorization and receipt atomically with D1 before posting.
