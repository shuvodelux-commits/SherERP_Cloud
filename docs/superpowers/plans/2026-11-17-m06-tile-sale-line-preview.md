## Design
One M06 internal `previewTileSaleLineCandidateD1({DB,productId,revision='current',request})` calls M03's existing **read-only** `proposeTileQuantityCandidateD1` and M02's exact `priceTileQuantity`. The DB binding is supplied by an authorized future server, **not the browser**. The caller cannot submit factor, length, width, unitPriceText, pricingUnit, stock authority, actor or tenant claims as trusted options. Only a product with an active, nondeleted, non-Bundle **current** unit/measurement revision can produce an indicative sale quote; old revisions remain readable for returns but must never be priced as a new sale. The source-priced `sellingPricingUnit` comes from D1 measurement and a trusted price from `catalog.sellingPriceText`, `unitConfig.stockSellingOverrideText`, `unitConfig.conversions[].sellingPriceOverrideText` or `sellingSqftPriceOverrideText`. If the chosen anchor unit is the same as the selected Box/Pcs unit, main price and any explicitly stored override must agree exactly; conflicting anchors, blank/invalid prices or inactive conversion fail with a stable `NEEDS_REVIEW` error. Preserve manufacturer evidence and current measurement revision. Never use a purchase price in POS or leak `catalog.purchasePriceText` in the returned preview.

The output is a minimal **private, read-only** response with `productId`, `productName`, `revision`, `stockUnit:'Pcs'`, `quantity` (including exact Box/Pcs/Sqft fractions, immutable measurement/unit snapshots) and `salePreview` (selected pricing unit/price, exact billed fraction, exact rounded line minor units, display rounding delta, `postingAllowed:false`), plus `stockAuthority:'not-loaded'`. A client **must never** derive line revenue by multiplying displayed 2-decimal sqft/box quantities; use `lineAmountMinor` calculated from the rational quantity. No stock check, FIFO/Cash Session, source ID issuance, split payments, journal, Party, ledger or D1 writes occur. M03 must independently verify current actor/tenant, stock lots, unit revision, receipt/stock/Cash/Bank/Party atomicity before any persisted sale.
